# RFID retrieval, provenance and comparison boundaries

Date: 2026-09-30

## Observation and scope

One authorized ordinary production assessment of battery-free RFID temperature
sensing for cold-chain logistics completed in 250 seconds with 14 sources.
The observed Crew estimate was USD 0.054203 and the separate auxiliary estimate
was USD 0.000779. Their sum, USD 0.054982, excludes search charges and is not a
provider invoice. Repeated terminal status/progress observations agreed, and
the browser displayed both accounting scopes. This was one successful normal
run, not a cancellation, recovery, PDF or hard-budget validation.

Successful delivery did not establish correct reasoning. Inspection found:

- two battery/electrolyte or battery-cluster patents admitted by generic terms;
- secondary financial-news pages labelled as first-party company pages; and
- a saved abstract describing a physical temperature difference reinterpreted
  in the report as sensor measurement error, then used to justify decision
  targets. This last finding compares saved source text and generated prose;
  it is not independent verification of the original paper.

The original report and recorded inputs remain unchanged. No further paid
assessment, supplementary search or experimental Tool Calling is authorized
or enabled by this repair.

## Deterministic retrieval and attribution repair

For patent-title relevance, `battery-free` is treated as a compound rather than
two positive matches for battery and free. A separate unnegated battery mention
still counts; genuine active/semi-active RFID temperature competitors remain
eligible through their other matching terms. This is not a general negation
parser or a rule that competing power architectures are irrelevant.

The same narrow check applies to Web and Lens patent admission. Existing
thresholds and scoring formulas are unchanged. The preserved-candidate replay
retains six of the eight observed patents, removing only the two motivating
off-topic records. All 240 saved patents in the 30-report, ten-topic frozen
corpus retain their old admission disposition.

Yahoo Finance and Investing.com are classified by hostname as third-party
financial news or syndicated content, not the announcing company's own site.
Unknown content-shaped URLs retain the existing `company_disclosure` candidate
category for compatibility, but their reason now explicitly says ownership
and first-party attribution are unverified. URL shape is not proof of company
identity, original authorship or claim truth. DNS-boundary controls prevent a
lookalike host from inheriting the known host classification.

The replay keeps all three observed market records and all 219 frozen market
records; 68 frozen records change their provenance explanation. All 31 replay
input files retain their hashes. This is an in-sample regression census of
preserved candidates, not new retrieval, independent accuracy, or proof that
every retained source is useful.

## Semantic mitigation, not a new verifier

The evidence guardrail already serializes saved source summaries alongside
model-authored findings. Writer and Reviewer receive those evidence task
outputs. No missing summary channel was established. The available saved
artifacts do not identify which historical model stage first introduced the
misinterpretation or rule out context reduction on that historical request.

Writer/Reviewer instructions now explicitly preserve the measured subject,
reference, units, conditions and negative caveats, checking saved summaries
as well as intermediate findings. Physical differences must not be relabelled
as calibration error. Corrections must address dependent claims, not merely
hedge a false premise. Legitimate separately labelled analyst experiment
proposals remain distinct from thresholds claimed to follow from evidence.

Scripted controls exercise source-to-context and prescribed-correction-to-final
delivery, including physical differences, genuine calibration error, mixed
quantities and negative caveats. They cannot prove that a native model will
choose the right interpretation or correction. No regex semantic classifier,
new blocking guardrail, scoring change or retrospective report rewrite is
introduced. Native prevention effectiveness remains unverified.

The observed Chinese report's English-only audit was correctly unavailable;
the numeric screen checked zero claims and left one unverifiable. Neither that
state nor a completed Reviewer constitutes an all-clear for factual accuracy.
